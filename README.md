# Trades website template

For carpenters, plumbers, electricians, painters, cleaners and landscapers. A phone button on the first screen, your numbers and reviews for trust, services with starting prices, how a job runs in three steps, the towns you serve, questions, and a quote request form. Structured data describes a local service business and its area.

[![Deploy to Statpio](https://img.shields.io/badge/Deploy%20to-Statpio-f39200?style=for-the-badge)](https://statpio.com/new?template=trades)

Live preview: https://statpio.com/templates/trades/preview · Template page: https://statpio.com/templates/trades

## What is in it

- One page, plain HTML and CSS: no framework, no build step, no scripts, nothing loaded from a third party.
- Sections: Hero, Trust numbers, Services, How it works, Reviews, Service area, Questions, Quote.
- A quote form that posts to the page's own address (on Statpio the messages land in your inbox; elsewhere point it at a form backend).
- Structured data (HomeAndConstructionBusiness and FAQPage), Open Graph tags, a canonical address, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, an llms.txt for AI assistants, a 404 page, a favicon and a web manifest.
- Its own fonts, self-hosted: archivo and public sans (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License, see fonts/OFL.txt).

## Use it

**On Statpio (free):** press the button above. The site goes live at your-name.onstatpio.com within seconds with the form working, and the Site content page lets you replace the demo texts and photos without touching the code.

**Anywhere else:** download or clone this repository and upload the folder to any static host. Edit index.html in a text editor; the colours are the `--primary` and `--accent` variables at the top of styles.css.
